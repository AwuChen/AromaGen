How this is calculated

The task

Each trial shows a participant one smell and 4 candidate descriptor words (option_a–option_d), one of which is correct (correct_index). The participant picks one (selected_index).

Two accuracy metrics

  • Exact accuracy — selected_index === correct_index. The participant named the precise word.
  • Class (family) accuracy — the cluster of the selected word matches the trial's target cluster, even if the exact word is wrong. Every descriptor is mapped to one of 12 odor families (Floral, Citrus, Woody & Resinous, Herbal & Cooling, Spice, Sweet & Gourmand, Roasted & Smoky, Fermented & Sour, Putrid & Decay, Body & Animalic, Chemical & Solvent, Perfumed & Clean) using the fixed-ratio-vector taxonomy. This credits "right family, wrong word" answers — e.g. selecting lavender when the target was rose still counts, since both are Floral.

Aggregates

Both metrics are rolled up per cluster, per smell (the correct descriptor), per participant, and per day (the date a participant's session was logged). The confidence/familiarity panel splits mean self-reported confidence_1to5 and familiarity_1to5_for_descriptor by whether the trial was correct, plus a Pearson correlation between each rating and correctness.

Data & persistence

user_study_result.csv is the single source of truth — click Update from user_study_result.csv any time it changes (new participant, corrected row, etc.) and the dashboard fully replaces its data with what's in that file. In between updates, the last-loaded data is cached in this browser's local storage so the dashboard isn't blank on reopen, but it will not sync across devices/browsers and can be cleared by clearing site data — use Download backup if you want a portable copy independent of the file.

Backup & restore

The dashboard's data mirrors whatever user_study_result.csv you last clicked "Update" with at the top of the page — analyze.py reads that same file directly, so the two naturally stay in sync. This backup/restore is just for this browser: download a backup after a session, and restore it here (or in a fresh browser) to pick up where you left off without re-selecting the file.
